# JD And Company Research

## Capture The Original

Save the complete job description, URL, requisition ID and capture date. If a URL cannot be read, ask for pasted text rather than reconstructing it from company reputation.

## Decode Japanese Hiring Terms

Extract and explain at least:

- 雇用形態: 正社員, 契約社員, 業務委託, 派遣
- 試用期間
- 業務内容 and 変更の範囲
- 就業場所 and 変更の範囲
- 勤務時間, フレックス, 裁量労働制, みなし労働時間
- 時間外労働 and 固定残業代
- 休日, 年間休日, 有給休暇
- salary components, allowances, bonus and review cycle
- 転勤, 出張, remote/hybrid requirements
- Japanese/English level
- residence and visa wording

Flag ambiguous words such as `応相談`, `会社の定める業務`, `会社の定める場所`, `みなし残業`, `ポテンシャル採用`, `即戦力`, `裁量`, and `アットホーム` for follow-up rather than assigning one fixed meaning.

## Company Research Pack

Research only what changes the decision or interview preparation:

1. legal entity and employing entity in Japan
2. product/business model and target customer
3. team and role purpose from official recruiting material
4. recent business or product events with dates
5. engineering/design/sales culture from first-party material
6. compensation and working-condition benchmarks
7. visa, language and location evidence
8. selection process evidence

Separate:

- **Fact**: supported directly by a source
- **Inference**: reasoned from facts/JD wording
- **Anecdote**: review/forum claim
- **Unknown**: ask recruiter or hiring manager

## Japan Decision Matrix

Assess these dimensions without claiming a hiring probability:

| Dimension | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Can the candidate legally and practically perform this work? |
| Core evidence | Are the highest-value responsibilities supported by real examples? |
| Language | Can the candidate interview and work at the required level? |
| Location | Is residence, commute, remote policy and transfer scope acceptable? |
| Compensation | Is total annual compensation competitive for this lane and candidate? |
| Work design | Are hours, overtime, contract, probation and autonomy acceptable? |
| Growth | Does the role improve skills, scope, network or future options? |
| Opportunity cost | Is preparation effort justified relative to alternatives? |

Output `strong / mixed / weak / unknown` with evidence for each dimension.

## Recruiter Questions

Prepare concise questions for unresolved material items:

- What language is used in interviews, documentation and daily meetings?
- Is Japan residence required at application or start date?
- What visa/status support can the employing entity provide?
- What are the role and work-location change scopes?
- How are base salary, fixed overtime, allowances, bonus and equity separated?
- What is the average and expected overtime for this team?
- What are the interview stages and evaluation criteria?
- Why is the role open, and what does success in 6-12 months look like?
