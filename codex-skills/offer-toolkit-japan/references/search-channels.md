# Japan Search Channels

Treat this as a routing map, not a permanent ranking. Verify that each service, filter, and application route is current before recommending it.

## Public And Official Support

- **Hello Work / foreign employment service centers**: consultation, referrals, seminars, interview events and support for international students/professionals.
- **JASSO**: international-student job-hunting guide and recruiting schedule guidance.
- **JETRO Open for Professionals**: companies interested in highly skilled foreign professionals, events and career information.
- **University career centers**: new-graduate schedules, alumni, recommendation routes and document review.

## New Graduate

Research current listings and deadlines on company recruiting pages first, then major new-graduate platforms such as Mynavi, Rikunavi and Career-tasu. Confirm graduation-year eligibility, pre-entry deadlines, internship type and whether the route accepts international applicants.

## Japanese Mid-Career

Use a mix of direct applications, referral networks, recruiting agents and current platforms such as doda, Recruit Agent, Mynavi Tenshoku, BizReach, Green and Wantedly where appropriate. Platform fit varies by seniority, profession and compensation; verify current terms and fees before recommending.

## Bilingual And International

- LinkedIn
- Daijob
- CareerCross
- JETRO OFP list and job fairs
- direct company career pages
- specialist recruiters, after checking representation and data-sharing terms

## English-Friendly Technology

- TokyoDev
- JapanDev
- direct careers pages for international engineering organizations
- relevant technical communities and referrals

Filter explicitly for:

- Japanese level
- Japan resident required
- apply from abroad
- visa support/sponsorship wording
- remote/hybrid and actual work location
- salary range and employment type
- seniority and years of experience

## Research Procedure

1. Build Japanese and English keyword sets for the same role.
2. Search official company pages and 2-4 appropriate channels.
3. Deduplicate by company, team and requisition ID.
4. Store source URL, captured date, salary, language, location, residence and visa wording.
5. Mark stale, reposted or agency-anonymized listings.
6. Prioritize a small evidence-backed pipeline over mass applications.

## Automation Boundaries

- Do not scrape behind login walls or reuse cookies without explicit permission.
- Do not bypass CAPTCHA, rate limits or platform controls.
- Do not auto-submit applications or send bulk outreach.
- Use `$career-ops` for pipeline tracking and batch evaluation, but retain human approval for every submission and message.
