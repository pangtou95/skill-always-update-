# Interview Claim Boundaries

The candidate must be impressive without becoming inaccurate. Japanese mid-career interviews often test the boundary between participation and ownership through repeated follow-ups.

## Role Ladder

Use the highest level supported by evidence.

| Level | Accurate claim | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | observed or studied | course, notes, lab or shadowing context |
| 1 | assisted | assigned task, reviewer and exact deliverable |
| 2 | owned a task | task scope, output and completion criteria |
| 3 | main implementer/担当 | requirements received, design/build/test scope and users |
| 4 | coordinated a workstream | stakeholders, dependencies, decisions escalated and result |
| 5 | formal PL/PM | charter, schedule/risk/budget authority, team and decision rights |
| 6 | people manager | direct reports, goal setting, evaluation, hiring or coaching authority |

Never convert level 3 or 4 into level 5. `主担当`, `実務推進`, `部門横断調整` and `PL/PM` are not synonyms.

### Safe Japanese

- `正式なPL・PMではありませんが、実務推進の担当として、関係者への確認と調整を行いました。`
- `直属のメンバーはいませんでした。部下の管理ではなく、複数部署との調整経験です。`
- `意思決定は責任者が行い、私は選択肢の整理と実行を担当しました。`

## Delivery Ladder

| Level | Label | Do not overstate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| study | 学習・検証 | no business use |
| prototype | prototype | feasibility only |
| PoC | PoC/技術検証 | scoped validation, not production |
| internal tool | 社内業務支援ツール | business use, but not necessarily a core system |
| production workflow | 実運用ワークフロー | actual users, operation and failure handling required |
| customer production system | 顧客向け本番システム | customer acceptance, production responsibility and support scope required |

Ask for actual users, use frequency, owner, monitoring, failure handling and change history before claiming `実運用`.

### Safe Japanese

- `基幹システムではなく、社内業務支援ツールです。`
- `一部は実運用ですが、別の機能はPoC段階です。`
- `要件確認、構成検討、構築、動作確認まで担当しました。正式な大規模開発工程ではありません。`

## AI-Assisted Work

Separate four things:

1. business requirement and problem definition;
2. human-owned design, decisions, verification and accountability;
3. AI-assisted research, drafting or implementation;
4. AI as a product feature or API in the delivered workflow.

Do not say `AIでシステムを作りました` when the accurate claim is that AI assisted research or implementation. Be ready to explain what would fail without the candidate's own judgment.

Current hiring-side research reports that many recruiters encounter AI-like applications and answers. Polished wording is not evidence. Preserve natural speech, concrete artifacts and reproducible reasoning.

## Certification Comparison

For a lesser-known vendor certification:

1. state the issuing organization and exact certification name;
2. state the covered domain and topics;
3. state level only as defined by the issuer;
4. explain overlap with a familiar certification without claiming equivalence;
5. connect the learning to a real task.

Safe pattern:

`これは[issuer]が認定する[domain]分野の[issuer-defined level]資格です。[topics]を扱います。[familiar certification]と扱う領域は近いですが、別のベンダー資格で、公式な同等認定があるとは申し上げません。`

Do not use `イコール`, `完全に同じ`, `同等レベル` or a qualification-framework mapping without an official source.

## Customer Contact

Distinguish:

- direct discovery with the customer;
- requirements received through a manager or sales person;
- external coordination with a regulator, vendor or auditor;
- internal stakeholder interviews;
- document submission without decision-making authority.

An external contact is not automatically a customer proposal. State the channel and decision rights.

## Project And Team Size

Record separately:

- total program size;
- immediate team size;
- number of people the candidate regularly coordinated;
- direct reports;
- candidate's own deliverables;
- reviewer and final approver.

If the candidate does not know total program size, say `全体規模は確認できていません` and give the known immediate-team facts.

## Results And Numbers

Classify every result:

- `verified number`: source exists;
- `observed outcome`: usage, approval, completion or error reduction is observable;
- `stakeholder feedback`: identify who and context;
- `candidate inference`: clearly label as inference;
- `unknown`: do not fill with a plausible number.

Numbers in submitted documents and spoken answers must match exactly.

## Short Tenure

Use this sequence:

1. objective fact and duration;
2. reason without attacking the employer;
3. improvement or consultation attempted;
4. candidate's own reflection, including pre-entry verification gaps when true;
5. what changed in the next decision process;
6. why the target role reduces recurrence risk.

Do not use youth, urgency or ambition alone to justify a very short stay. The interviewer is testing judgment, learning and retention risk.

## Career Consistency

Build a single evidence-backed axis such as `network -> cloud -> security/automation -> customer delivery`. Do not erase changes; explain how each move expanded or corrected the axis.

For every move, answer:

- what capability was sought;
- why that company or contract was chosen;
- what was actually gained;
- why the next move followed;
- what remains consistent now.

## Answer Card

For high-risk questions, create:

- one-sentence conclusion;
- facts and timeline;
- candidate's exact role;
- evidence or artifact;
- result and limitation;
- connection to target role;
- likely follow-ups;
- `20s`, `60s`, and `120s` spoken versions.

Do not force `理由は2点あります` when one reason is more truthful. The answer should sound spoken, not generated.
