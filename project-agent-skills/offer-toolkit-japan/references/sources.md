## Source Discipline

- Cite direct pages, not search-result URLs.
- Record publication/update date when visible.
- Prefer current official guidance over old blog posts.
- For interview intelligence, record both the interview event date and the page publication/update date.
- Treat recruiter blogs and salary guides as commercial sources and identify their incentive.
- Treat moderated candidate reports as stage-specific observations, not company-wide rules.
- Treat forums and reviews as anecdotal leads requiring corroboration.
- Paraphrase question patterns instead of copying long candidate answers.
- Never present this skill as legal, immigration, tax or labor-law advice.
