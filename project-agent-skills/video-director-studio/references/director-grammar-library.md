# Director Grammar Library

Choose one primary grammar and at most one accent grammar. A grammar is a production system, not a pile of adjectives.

## Narrative And Film

| ID | Profile | Craft contract | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `G-OBS-DOMESTIC` | Domestic observational realism | Eye-level 35-50mm, available-light logic, lived-in blocking, pauses and small gestures, patient cuts, room tone | sentimental scoring, constant close-ups, beautified interiors |
| `G-FORMAL-STORYBOOK` | Formal storybook precision | Frontal/symmetrical frames, planar movement, centered blocking, controlled palette, graphic match cuts, dry music cues | random handheld motion, uncontrolled clutter, generic whimsy |
| `G-NEON-FRAGMENT` | Neon fragmented romance | Tight fragments, reflective glass, practical neon, shallow focus, off-speed motion accents, ellipses, voice or music memory bridges | copying a specific film, incoherent geography, color without motivation |
| `G-YOUTH-LYRICAL` | Youthful lyrical backlight | Airy wide/medium frames, windows and seasonal light, gentle handheld or slider, tactile details, emotional reaction cuts | glamour posing, excessive bloom, empty slow motion |
| `G-SOCIAL-DISTANCE` | Austere social distance | Static wide frames, architectural separation, long holds, restrained coverage, sparse non-diegetic music | dramatic push-ins, overexplained emotion, decorative montage |
| `G-URBAN-BLOCKING` | Procedural urban blocking | Strong entrances/exits, foreground occlusion, group geometry, practical hard light, 180-degree discipline, decisive cuts | floating characters, axis breaks without purpose, vague staging |
| `G-STANDOFF` | Stillness-to-impact tension | Extreme detail against wide geography, held silence, preparation and eyelines, sudden action, short decay | constant action, weightless impacts, full-frame shake |
| `G-AUTEUR-UNCANNY` | Everyday uncanny realism | Ordinary textures plus one impossible rule, restrained camera, precise sound intrusion, unresolved visual residue | random surreal objects, dream haze everywhere, no causal rule |
| `G-SILENT-PHYSICAL` | Silent physical comedy | Clear goal/obstacle/prop, readable full-body staging, setup-escalation-reversal, locked or motivated follow, musical Foley | dialogue-dependent joke, hidden action, cutting before payoff |
| `G-OBS-DOC` | Observational documentary | Real location anchors, respectful distance, motivated handheld, process detail, ambient sound, evidence-led sequence | staged reactions presented as real, intrusive camera tricks |
| `G-HUMAN-MEMORY` | Old-city humanist memory | Place-led establishing shots, faces and hands, worn textures, restrained nostalgia, sound bridges through space | heritage postcard montage, sepia-only nostalgia, invented facts |
| `G-GOLDEN-OPULENCE` | Golden-age public/private contrast | Geometric public spectacle versus intimate private frames, warm practicals, wide lenses for rooms, selective close detail | gold on every surface, costume without social context |
| `G-BRUTAL-MONUMENT` | Monumental brutalism | Human-to-architecture scale contrast, hard geometry, overcast or raking light, slow axial moves, low-frequency sound | generic dystopia, tiny unreadable subject, excessive fog |
| `G-BLUE-NATURAL` | Cool-blue naturalism | Blue-hour source logic, realistic skin, damp streets/windows, micro-actions, breathing handheld, low-key music | cyan skin, influencer posing, strong soap-opera performance |
| `G-ANALOG-LIFESTYLE` | Analog nostalgic lifestyle | Practical objects, imperfect framing, restrained grain, tactile inserts, simple pans/zooms, period-consistent sound | fake damage overlays, mixed periods, unreadable product |

## Selection Method

1. Select the grammar that best serves the audience response and format.
2. Copy its craft contract into structured fields, not as a single style phrase.
3. Add one accent only if it changes a specific dimension. Example: `G-TVC-STORY` primary plus `G-CRYSTAL-DREAM` material accent.
4. Record conflicts. `G-OBS-DOC` and `G-HYPER-PRODUCT` should not both control camera and edit rhythm.
5. Turn every selected trait into a shot-level decision or remove it.
