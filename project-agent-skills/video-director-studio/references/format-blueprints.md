## Duration Planning

### Five-second proof

One subject, one action, one camera move, one material/light change, one stable endpoint. Use it to test identity, product geometry, motion physics, and the primary grammar.

### Fifteen-second social/TVC

- `0-2s`: recognizable hook or outcome preview;
- `2-6s`: setup and first proof;
- `6-11s`: main demonstration, turn, or peak;
- `11-15s`: payoff, product/name, CTA, or emotional button.

Do not treat these as four mandatory shots. A single continuous shot may contain the structure if physical continuity is stronger.

### Thirty-to-sixty seconds

Use 6-12 functional shots. Alternate information scale: geography -> action -> detail -> reaction -> proof. Reserve enough time for dialogue comprehension and a readable end frame.

### Multi-minute work

Plan sequence and scene states first. Generate shot-sized clips, accept or reject each, and update continuity from observed footage. Never ask one generation to carry the entire narrative.

## Platform Overlay

- `9:16`: protect face/product/text safe zones; movement must read on a narrow frame.
- `16:9`: use geography and lateral blocking; do not leave the subject tiny without intent.
- `1:1` or `4:5`: simplify horizontal staging and preserve top/bottom copy space.
- Feed autoplay: the opening must work muted; sound should add meaning, not rescue a blank frame.
- Presentation/website: allow slower reveal but ensure the first frame identifies the subject or product.
